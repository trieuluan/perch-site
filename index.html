Perch

A small hub that lives in your MacBook's notch

Music with visualizers and synced lyrics, system stats, calendar reminders, the menu bar icons the notch hides, and quick alerts, all around the camera.

Download for macOS

Free & open source · macOS 14 Sonoma or later

$brew install --cask trieuluan/perch/perch

Everything around the camera

Visualizers

Visualizers that hear the music

Perch analyzes what your Mac is playing, on-device, and follows 24 frequency bands, the lead melody, drum hits and how bright the sound is. It shows up behind the player (7 styles), next to the album art in the closed notch, and across the menu bar (11 styles), in colors from the album art or your own gradient.

Synced lyrics

Sing along from the notch

The line being sung shows under the closed notch, and long lines glide sideways so you can read them to the end. Open the notch and swipe the player with two fingers to see the whole song, karaoke style: click a line to jump there, or nudge the timing when a music video runs early. Lyrics come from LRCLIB, with Vietnamese titles and YouTube-style names handled.

Notch alerts

Quiet alerts beside the camera

Short messages when something happens: an event is about to start, a reminder is due, the Mac is overloaded (and which app is responsible), headphones connect (with battery), the keyboard input source changes, or Caps Lock turns on.

Calendar

Join meetings from the notch

A few minutes before a meeting, the open notch shows a pill with the time left. Click it to join Google Meet, Zoom, Microsoft Teams, Webex, Slack huddles or FaceTime, in the native app when it's installed.

System at a glance

The System tab shows CPU, memory, network and battery with live sparklines.

Icons hidden by the notch

On a crowded menu bar, macOS hides icons behind the notch. The Menu Bar tab lists them, with their real icons, and opens their menus.

Audio output picker

Switch between speakers, AirPods and other outputs from a button in the player.

Perch also keeps everything from Boring Notch: the music player, calendar, file shelf, system HUDs, battery and webcam mirror. It's available in English and Vietnamese, following your Mac's language.

Install

Requires macOS 14 Sonoma or later (visualizers need 14.2+). After the first launch, Perch updates itself.

Recommended

With Homebrew

$brew install --cask trieuluan/perch/perch

The cask clears the quarantine flag for you, so Perch opens straight away.

Or download the DMG

  1. Download Perch.dmg and drag Perch into Applications.
  2. Open Perch. macOS says it can't verify the developer, because Perch isn't notarized by Apple yet. Click Done.
  3. Go to System Settings › Privacy & Security, scroll down to the message about Perch, and click Open Anyway.
  4. Confirm with your password or Touch ID. You only do this once.

Prefer the Terminal? This does the same thing:

$xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Perch.app

Permissions Perch asks for

System Audio Recording
Visualizers. Audio is analyzed on-device, never recorded or sent anywhere. Without it, visualizers fall back to a simple animation.
Accessibility
Showing and opening menu bar icons hidden by the notch.
Calendars & Reminders
The calendar, event reminders and the Join button.
